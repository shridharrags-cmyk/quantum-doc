## Sources for factual claims

- Google Quantum AI, *Meet Willow, our state-of-the-art quantum chip* (Dec 2024): 105 qubits, below-threshold surface code, 10^25-year random-circuit-sampling claim.
- Google Quantum AI / Nature 638, 920–926 (2025): logical error per cycle 0.143% at distance 7, suppression factor Λ = 2.14 per +2 distance.
- IBM Quantum, *Development roadmap to 2033*: Starling, 2029, 200 logical qubits, 100 million gates.
- Microsoft, *Majorana 1* (Feb 2025) and the accompanying Nature paper; AWS Ocelot cat-qubit announcement (Feb 2025).
- NIST, FIPS 203 / 204 / 205 (Aug 2024): ML-KEM, ML-DSA, SLH-DSA.
- Arute et al., *Quantum supremacy using a programmable superconducting processor*, Nature 574, 505–510 (2019); IBM's 2.5-day counter-analysis.
- Landauer, *Irreversibility and Heat Generation in the Computing Process*, IBM J. Res. Dev. 5 (1961); Bennett (1973); Benioff (1980); Feynman (1981); Deutsch (1985); Shor (1994).
- Gidney & Ekerå, *How to factor 2048 bit RSA integers in 8 hours using 20 million noisy qubits* (2019).
- Bluvstein et al., *Logical quantum processor based on reconfigurable atom arrays*, Nature (Dec 2023): 48 logical qubits.
